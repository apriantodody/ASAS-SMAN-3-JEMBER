Soal ujian

Answer the following questions clearly and correctly!

1.
Read the dialogue below.

Sinta : I think English is an important subject for our future.

Bayu : I agree with you. We need it to get a good job.

Rina : I'm not sure about that. Some jobs don't need English.

Write the expressions that show (a) giving an opinion, (b) agreeing, and (c) disagreeing!

2.
Complete the dialogue with suitable expressions of opinion, agreement, and disagreement.

Dika : ______ (opinion) online shopping is more practical than going to the mall.

Lia : ______ (agreement), because we can compare prices easily.

Omar : ______ (disagreement), because the goods may not look like the pictures.

Write one suitable expression for each blank!

3.
Read the Instagram post below.

@smansa_bazaar: "School Bazaar 2026! Come and enjoy food, games, and live music. Saturday, 21 November, 8 AM - 3 PM at the school field. Entrance is free! #SchoolBazaar #SmansaFest"

Answer the questions:

(a) What is the purpose of the post?

(b) When and where will the event be held?

(c) Who is the post addressed to?

4.
Read the post below.

@momo_lover: "LOST! Orange cat named Momo near Jl. Melati yesterday. Please call 0812-3456-7890 if you see him. Thank you! #LostCat #HelpMeFindMomo"

Analyze the post:

(a) What kind of post is it and what is its purpose?

(b) Write two language features used in the post, with examples!

5.
Your English Club is looking for new members.

Details: Friday, 20 November - Room 12 - 2 PM - free snacks.

Write an Instagram caption (30-50 words) to invite your friends to join. Include the three details above, one call to action, and one hashtag. Then, write two sentences that you would say when presenting your caption in class!

6.
Complete the paragraph with the expressions in the box.

Lake Toba (1) ______ North Sumatra. It is (2) ______ its huge size and Samosir Island. The view of the lake is (3) ______, and the atmosphere is (4) ______.

Write the correct expression for each number!

7.
Think about your favorite place (a beach, a park, a mall, your hometown, etc.).

Describe the place in 3-4 sentences. Use at least three expressions or phrases to describe a place (e.g. "is located in", "is surrounded by", "is famous for", "breathtaking")!

8.
Read the text below.

Lake Toba

Lake Toba is the largest volcanic lake in Indonesia. It is located in North Sumatra. The lake is very wide and the water is clear and cool. In the middle of the lake, there is Samosir Island. The island has green hills, traditional Batak houses, and friendly people. Many tourists visit Lake Toba because of its beautiful scenery.

Answer the questions:

(a) What is the text about?

(b) Which sentences are the identification?

(c) Write two adjectives used to describe the lake!

9.
Read the statement below.

"Students should be allowed to bring smartphones to school."

Do you agree or disagree? Write your opinion and defend it with two reasons in 40-60 words!

10.
Read the text below.

Reading Should Be a Daily Habit

Reading should be a daily habit for every student. First, reading improves our knowledge. By reading books and articles, we learn new things about the world. Second, reading helps us to write and speak better because we find many new words and sentence patterns. In conclusion, students should read every day to become smarter and more confident.

Identify (a) the thesis, (b) two arguments, and (c) the reiteration of the text!
